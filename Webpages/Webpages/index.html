Welcome to my Portfolio

I am Gbarakoro Te ee dee Nwiiyee, a technology entrepreneur, software developer, digital solutions creator, and innovation enthusiast passionate about leveraging technology to solve real-world problems. I am the founder and leader of Matech Incorporated, a technology consulting and digital solutions company focused on helping businesses, organisations, and individuals embrace digital transformation through innovative, affordable, and reliable technology solutions. My interests span software development, web and mobile application development, artificial intelligence, automation, UI/UX design, digital marketing, business technology, and technology education. Beyond building digital products, I am passionate about empowering individuals, especially young people, with practical technology skills that prepare them for opportunities in the digital economy. I believe technology should not just be about creating products; it should be about solving problems, improving lives, creating opportunities, and building a better future.

My Personal Mission

To leverage technology, creativity, and innovation to develop practical solutions that solve problems, empower people, and create opportunities for businesses and individuals.

My Personal Vision

To become a recognised technology innovator and entrepreneur contributing to Africa's digital transformation through software development, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship, and technology education.

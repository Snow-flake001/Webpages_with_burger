PROJECTS AND INNOVATIONS

Some of the projects and initiatives I am developing or supporting include:

STYLEHUB – Fashion Marketplace

StyleHub is a fashion marketplace concept designed to connect fashion designers and customers through a digital platform. The platform aims to support:

  • Fashion designers.
  • Ready-made clothing sales.
  • Custom-made clothing orders.
  • Buyer and designer accounts.
  • Digital fashion commerce.

BIZ-BUZ – Business Advertising Platform

BIZ-BUZ is an advertising platform concept designed to help businesses promote their products and services digitally. Its proposed features include:

  • Business registration.
  • Product and service advertisements.
  • Subscription-based advertising plans.
  • Social media advertising integration.
  • Business visibility and promotion.
  • Advertising campaign management.

KIDS AND TEENS TECH CLUB

A technology education initiative designed to introduce children and teenagers to technology through practical learning. Training areas include:

  • Introduction to Coding.
  • Scratch Programming.
  • Game Development.
  • Robotics and STEM.
  • Graphics Design.
  • Digital Creativity.
  • Artificial Intelligence.

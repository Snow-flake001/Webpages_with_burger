ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Gbarakoro Te ee dee Nwiiyee is a technology entrepreneur, software developer, and digital solutions creator based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. He leads Matech Incorporated, a technology consulting and digital solutions company focused on software development, AI automation, product design, and technology education. He is passionate about innovation, entrepreneurship, and empowering individuals through technology. Gbarakoro Te ee dee Nwiiyee is an emerging technology entrepreneur and digital innovation enthusiast committed to leveraging technology to solve problems and create opportunities. As the leader of Matech Incorporated, he is focused on developing digital solutions that support business growth, improve productivity, and make technology more accessible. His professional interests include software engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, product design, digital transformation, and technology education. Beyond entrepreneurship, he is passionate about equipping young people with relevant digital skills through practical training initiatives such as Matech Tech Academy and the Kids and Teens Tech Club. His long-term ambition is to contribute meaningfully to Africa's technology ecosystem by building innovative products, empowering people, and creating sustainable digital opportunities.

My professional activities and solutions are directed towards:
